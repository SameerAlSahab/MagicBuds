Galaxy Wearable Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 1, 2021

At Samsung Electronics Co., Ltd., we know how important privacy is to our customers. Samsung is the data controller for your Galaxy Wearable. We have created this Privacy Notice to ensure you understand how we collect and use your personal information. 

Our Privacy Policy - which can be viewed at https://account.samsung.com/membership/terms/privacypolicy - also applies to your use of your Galaxy Wearable. Our Privacy Policy contains more information about how we use your data. Please read our Privacy Policy in addition to this Privacy Notice. However, this Privacy Notice at all times prevails over the Privacy Policy.

WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Through your Galaxy Wearable, we collect and maintain information about you in various ways. 

Information You Provide Directly

• When you log-in to Samsung account on your Galaxy Wearable device, Samsung account information, such as your Samsung account ID.

Information About Your Use of Your Galaxy Wearable

• Information about your devices, such as device type, device model, OS type and version, device identifiers and other unique identifiers, mobile network providers, device configurations and settings;
• Information related to your location through, GPS, Bluetooth and WiFi signals. Some features of your Galaxy Wearable require the collection of your device's precise geolocation, and in those instances we will collect your geolocation, but only with your permission. Your device will notify you when a Galaxy Wearable feature needs to collect your precise geolocation. Please note that if you do not allow Galaxy Wearable to collect your precise geolocation, you may not be able to use all of the features of Galaxy Wearable; 
• If your device offers a Remote Connection feature (available on Galaxy Watch3 and earlier models), such as connecting your mobile device to your Galaxy Wearable device over Wi-Fi or mobile data rather than Bluetooth so your Galaxy Wearable can receive messages from your phone, information about apps installed on your mobile device such as notifications and content of received messages and emails (including the content of messages and media files); and  
• If you use the Backup and Restore feature; software version, sales code, list of installed and recently used apps, data and settings of installed apps, home screen settings including watch face, apps and widgets, notification settings, system settings including display, sound and vibration.
• If you have agreed to sending diagnostic data, diagnostic, technical, error, and usage information such as the time and duration of your use of your Galaxy Wearable, apps installed by you and how long and when you open or close apps.

We also may collect other information about you, your Galaxy Wearable and apps, your devices connected to Galaxy Wearable, and your use of your Galaxy Wearable in ways that we describe to you at the time of collection and whenever is necessary, we will obtain your consent.

HOW DO WE USE YOUR INFORMATION?

• To help you register for and use your Galaxy Wearable. 
• To provide services and features you request. 
• To respond to your requests and questions, and to communicate with you about your Galaxy Wearable, including providing assistance when you request technical support related to your Galaxy Wearable. 
• To allow you to interact with selected Samsung or third-party apps, features and services. For example, you may be able to back up your apps and settings information to Samsung Cloud or use Samsung Health on your Galaxy Wearable.
• To provide customized content and services related to your Galaxy Wearable. 
• To provide promotions and offers through direct marketing communications (such as via push notifications, emails, and text messages), only where you have given us your separate consent.
• To operate, evaluate and improve Galaxy Wearable and our business (including developing new products and services, enhancing and improving our products and services; managing our communications; analyzing our products, services and customer base; conducting marketing research; performing data analytics; and performing accounting, auditing and other internal functions).
• To ask you for your opinions about our products and services and to perform customer surveys; if so required, with your separate consent.
• To provide software updates, maintenance services, and support for your Galaxy Wearable.
• To protect against, identify and prevent fraud and other criminal activity, claims and other liabilities.
• To comply with and enforce applicable legal requirements, relevant industry standards and our policies, including this Privacy Notice and the Privacy Policy.

Your Galaxy Wearable may provide the ability to connect to or use other Samsung or third-party apps, services and features. For example, you may be able to use Samsung Health or Bixby Voice on your Galaxy Wearable device. These Samsung and third-party apps, services and features may have their own privacy notices. Review them to ensure that you understand how those services process your information. To the extent that any other linked or shown app, service, or other feature is not owned or controlled by us, we are not responsible for its content, use, or privacy practices.

Samsung processes personal information for the purposes described above. Samsung's legal basis to process personal information is outlined below: 

Keeping our promise to you (performance of contract, GDPR Article 6(1)(b))

• Registering you and your Galaxy Wearable
• Providing you with Galaxy Wearable, such as allowing you to back up your apps and settings information to cloud servers
• Allowing you to interact with other Samsung and third-party content

To promote our business interests (legitimate interest, GDPR Article 6(1)(f))

• Operating, evaluating, and improving Galaxy Wearable and our business
• Providing you with customized content
• Responding to your requests, instructions and inquiries
• Maintaining adequate security measures, support and updates
• Protecting against liability, including complying with industry standards and enforcing our policies

To comply with the law and legal processes (GDPR Article 6(1)(c))
• Complying with applicable statutes and regulatory and court orders

With your consent (GDPR Article 6(1)(a))
• Provide marketing notifications (such as via push notifications, emails, and text messages)
• Processing of diagnostic data
• Processing of geolocation data

WHO DO WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION WITH?

We will disclose your information internally within our business and to the following entities, but only for the purposes described above.
* Affiliates: other Samsung Electronics Group companies which we control or own;
* Service providers: carefully selected companies that provide services for or on behalf of us, such as data servers that maintain data on our behalf. These providers are also committed to protecting your information;
* Other parties when required by law or as necessary to protect your Galaxy Wearable: for example, it may be necessary by law, legal process, or court order from governmental authorities to disclose your information. They may also seek your information from us for the purposes of law enforcement, national security, anti-terrorism, or other issues that are related to public security;
* Other parties in connection with corporate transactions: we may disclose your information to a third party as part of a merger or transfer, acquisition or sale, or in the event of bankruptcy; and
* Other parties with your consent or at your direction: in addition to the disclosures described in this Privacy Notice, we may share information about you with third parties when you separately consent to or request such sharing.

HOW LONG DO WE RETAIN YOUR INFORMATION AND WHERE DOES IT GO?

How long your data will be retained depends on the legal basis relied upon to process your data. For example, data we process to perform our contract with you requires us to keep the data throughout the time you use your Galaxy Wearable. As long as you are an active user of your Galaxy Wearable, we will retain and process this data. Data such as device information and identifiers fall into this category.

Data we process to promote our business interests, such as your usage of your Galaxy Wearable, is only kept for as long as needed for the purposes for which it was collected. For example, this data may be collected to perform analytics so we can develop improvements to Galaxy Wearable, or we may process data to keep a record of inquiries you made through or about your Galaxy Wearable to improve your customer service experience.

In addition, we may also process your data based on your consent. If you withdraw your consent, we will stop processing your data that relies on your consent, but it will not impact the processing of data collected prior to your withdrawal until the purposes for which such data was collected have been achieved.

Please note, although we aim to retain your data for the time period described above, your data may be processed for longer pursuant to applicable law. For example, if a specific statute mandates that we require a certain piece of data, we will comply and retain that data until the required retention period expires. 

As long as your data is retained by us your data will always be subject to appropriate safeguards.

Your use of your Galaxy Wearable will involve the transfer, storage, and processing of your personal information to other countries; such countries include, without limitation, countries in the European Economic Area, the Republic of Korea, India and Vietnam. All international data transfers are subject to legal requirements to ensure that your personal information is processed safely and as you would expect. Such measures include the use of Standard Contractual Clauses to safeguard the transfer of data outside of the EEA. To request more information or to obtain a copy of the Standard Contractual Clauses in place, please see the CONTACT US section of the Privacy Notice.

HOW DO WE KEEP YOUR INFORMATION SECURE?

We take data protection very seriously. We have put in place physical and technical safeguards to keep the information we collect secure. However, please note that although we take reasonable steps to protect your information, no website, internet transmission, computer system, or wireless connection is completely secure.

WHAT ARE YOUR RIGHTS?

Your personal information belongs to you. You can ask us to provide details about what we've collected, and you can ask us to delete it or correct any inaccuracies. You can also ask us to restrict or limit processing, sharing, or transfer of your personal information, as well as to provide to you your personal information that we've collected so you can use it for your own purposes. However, requesting the deletion or other right related to restriction or limitation of processing, sharing or transfer of your personal information may also result in a loss of access, in whole or in part, to your Galaxy Wearable. We won't delete data that we're required by law to retain. 

To make a request concerning your rights or to make an inquiry, contact us. See the CONTACT US section.
