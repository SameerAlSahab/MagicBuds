2.Extended Feature– Send diagnostic data to Samsung
Before you use the Send diagnostic data to Samsung feature of Galaxy Wearable, please carefully read the details listed below.

If you disagree to our collection and use of your personal information, the Send diagnostic data to Samsung feature of Galaxy Wearable will not function properly and we will not be able to provide you with these functions. However, you can use other basic features properly. When you require the Send diagnostic data to Samsung feature, we will ask for your consent again.

If you want to withdraw your consent for the collection and use of personal information when you use the Product (or Service), open Galaxy Wearable, choose **Settings** > **About** > **Legal information**, and disable **Send diagnostic data to Samsung**.
