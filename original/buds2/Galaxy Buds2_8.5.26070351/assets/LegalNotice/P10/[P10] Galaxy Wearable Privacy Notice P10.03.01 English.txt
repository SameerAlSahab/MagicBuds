WHAT INFORMATION DO WE COLLECT?

Through Galaxy Wearable, we obtain and maintain information about you, including your basic personal data and sensitive personal data as defined by the law of Vietnam (hereinafter referred to as the "Applicable Law”) in various ways. 

Information You Provide Directly

• When you log in to Samsung account on your Galaxy Wearable device, we may collect Samsung account information, such as your Samsung account ID.

Information About Your Use of Galaxy Wearable

In addition to the information you provide, we will collect information about your use of our Galaxy Wearable through software on your devices and by other means. We will collect: 

• Information about your devices, such as device type, device model, OS type and version, device identifiers and other unique identifiers, mobile network providers, device configurations and settings;
• Information related to your location through, GPS, Bluetooth and Wi-Fi signals: Some features of your Galaxy Wearable require the collection of your device’s precise geolocation, and in those instances, we will collect your geolocation, but only with your permission. Your device will notify you when a Galaxy Wearable feature needs to collect your precise geolocation. Please note that if you do not allow Galaxy Wearable to collect your precise geolocation, you may not be able to use all of the features of Galaxy Wearable. 
• If your device offers a Remote Connection feature (available on Galaxy Watch3 and earlier models), such as connecting your mobile device to your Galaxy Wearable device over Wi-Fi or mobile data rather than Bluetooth so your Galaxy Wearable can receive messages from your phone: information about apps installed on your mobile device such as notifications and content of received messages and emails (including the content of messages and media files) 
• If you use the Backup and Restore feature: software version, sales code, list of installed and recently used apps, data and settings of installed apps, home screen settings including watch face, apps and widgets, notification settings, system settings including display, sound and vibration
• If you have agreed to sending diagnostic data: diagnostic, technical, error, and usage information such as the time and duration of your use of your Galaxy Wearable, apps installed by you and how long and when you open or close apps

We also may collect other information about you, your devices and apps, and your use of Galaxy Wearable in ways that we describe to you at the time of collection or otherwise with your consent.
